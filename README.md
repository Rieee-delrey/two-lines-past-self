A two-line warning to your past self for make.fun's Two Lines to Your Past Self bounty. The program writes exactly two lines to /output/entry.txt: line one warns about one specific moment, line two says in one sentence why that moment mattered and what changed because of it. 183 characters, inside the 1–280 gate.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
